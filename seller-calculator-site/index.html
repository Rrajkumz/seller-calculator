Seller fee questions

How much does Etsy take from each sale?

For a typical US sale: a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing. That is about 10% before advertising. Offsite Ads adds 12% to 15% on sales it drives.

How is the Amazon referral fee calculated?

It is a percentage of the total sale price, 15% in most categories, with a $0.30 minimum per item. FBA fulfillment fees are charged on top of it.

Why is using a third-party gateway on Shopify more expensive?

Shopify charges an extra 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and 0.6% on Advanced when you skip Shopify Payments, on top of whatever your gateway charges.
