FAQ

How much does Etsy take from each sale?

For a typical US sale: a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing. That is roughly 10% before advertising. Offsite Ads adds 12% to 15% on sales it drives.

How is the Amazon referral fee calculated?

It is a percentage of the total sale price, 15% in most categories, with a $0.30 minimum per item. FBA fulfillment fees and storage are charged on top of it.

Why is a third-party gateway more expensive on Shopify?

Shopify adds an extra 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and 0.6% on Advanced when you do not use Shopify Payments, on top of your gateway’s own fees.

Does the calculator include sales tax or monthly plan costs?

No. Sales tax, Shopify monthly plans and Amazon’s Professional subscription are not included in the per-sale results.

How accurate are the results?

They are estimates based on published rates. Fees vary by country, category and size, so confirm with each platform.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. Calculations happen in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to or stored on our servers.

What profit margin should I aim for?

It depends on your product and costs. Enter a target margin in the calculator and it shows the price needed to reach it after fees, shipping and ads.
